## 2. Market regime, Apr–Sep 2026

The 6-month window starts at the bottom of a war-driven Q1 selloff and ends in a rate shock. Sector leadership changed with each phase:

1. **Q1 shock (context).** US/Israeli strikes on Iran began Feb 28. Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz and oil spiked. Software sold off hard on AI-disruption fears (IGV −24% in Q1, its worst quarter since 2008). [Wikipedia: 2026 Iran war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war), [Yahoo/24/7: IGV](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-crushed-software-stocks-igv-194541393.html)
2. **Apr–May snap-back.** A ceasefire (Apr 7–8) and easing oil fed a violent high-beta rally: SPY +12.6% and XLK +24.8% between Mar 30 and Apr 29. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 had their best quarter since Q2 2020, and oil fell back toward pre-crisis levels. [Nasdaq Q2 review](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/june-second-quarter-2026-review-and-outlook)
3. **Jun–Jul rotation.** Semis peaked in late June, and chip stocks shed more than $1T in a July selloff. The ceasefire collapsed on **Jul 8** and energy jumped (+12% in July). Health care and financials took leadership. [CNBC: chip selloff](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/chip-selloff-sk-hynix-samsung-softbank.html), [Marks Group July recap](https://marksgroup.com/july-2026-market-recap/)
4. **Aug–Sep rate shock.** August CPI rose 0.4% m/m (3.4% y/y), driven by gasoline (+27% y/y). The **Fed hiked 25bp to 3.75–4.00% on Sep 16**, its first hike since 2023. The 10-yr reached ~5.2–5.3% (highest since 2007) and the 30-yr ~5.5–5.6% (highest since the early 2000s). Futures price ~70% odds of another hike in October. [CNBC: Fed](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html), [CNBC: CPI](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/cpi-inflation-report-august-2026.html), [Axios: yields](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/24/treasury-yields-inflation-bonds), [CNBC Sep 29](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)

**What the regime implies for sector evidence:**

- **Earnings are carrying the market.** FactSet's late-September estimate for Q3 S&P 500 EPS growth is ~29%, led by Energy (+111%). Upward revisions are led by Energy, IT and Financials; downward revisions by Materials, Staples and Health Care. [FactSet Earnings Insight](https://www.factset.com/earningsinsight)
- **Valuation leaves no rate cushion.** At ~19.3× forward EPS, the S&P 500 earnings yield (~5.2%) roughly equals the 10-yr Treasury yield. [StreetStats](https://streetstats.finance/valuation/market) Rate-sensitive, bond-proxy sectors are losing on every horizon.
- **Leadership is extremely narrow.** September: SPY −0.1% but the equal-weight RSP −4.2%. Only 3 of 11 sectors rose (Tech, Health Care, Comm Svcs). Over 6M, SPY beat RSP by 9.3pp. Over 3M the Magnificent-7 ETF (MAGS) gained +12.4% vs RSP −1.3%.
- **Positioning.** The September BofA FMS shows managers net 48% underweight bonds and staples net 33% underweight (the most bearish reading since 2004). Composite sentiment fell to 7.0 from 8.0, and managers are rotating toward health care. [Hedge Fund Tips summary](https://www.hedgefundtips.com/september-2026-bank-of-america-global-fund-manager-survey-results-summary/)

## 4. #1 Health Care: the most unusual relative strength

### Why it is unusual

A low-volatility, defensive sector has produced the largest 4-month excess return of any sector. It did so in a risk-on, earnings-led tape while bond yields rose to multi-decade highs. Rate-sensitive defensives (utilities, REITs, staples) all fell over the same period. The move has also been broad rather than led by one stock.

### Price evidence

**Magnitude**

- XLV +15.2% since May 29 vs SPY +1.5% (+13.6pp).
- XLV +20.5% since Apr 29 vs SPY +7.9% (+12.6pp).
- 12M: +27.7% vs SPY +16.4%.

**Shape**

- The relative-strength line bottomed on May 29 and is up 13.5% since.
- XLV beat SPY by 9.8pp in the Jun segment and 5.0pp in the Jul segment.
- It then consolidated rather than reversing: −2.6pp in Aug, +0.6pp in Sep.
- XLV is at its period high on the monthly-sampled path.

**Breadth**

- The equal-weight sector (RSPH) gained +11.9% over 3M vs RSP −1.3% (+13.2pp).
- RSPH beat XLV over 3M and 6M. Health care is the only sector where equal-weight beats cap-weight over both windows.

**Resilience**

- In September, XLV was one of only three sectors up, as the 10-yr pushed past 5%.
- It achieved this with 17% annualized volatility vs 30% for XLK.

### Catalysts, most of them structural rather than cyclical

- **Policy overhang cleared.** Drug-pricing (TrumpRx/most-favored-nation) and pharma tariff terms were largely settled via company agreements. Analysts credit that clarity for the rebound. [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/healthcare-increased-clarity-drug-pricing-tariffs-led-healthcare-rebound)
- **Managed care repriced.** CMS finalized a +2.48% Medicare Advantage rate for 2027, against +0.09% proposed. UNH rose ~11% and HUM ~9% on the day (Apr 6–7), with ~$13B of added insurer revenue. [STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2026/04/06/medicare-advantage-rates-2027-unitedhealth-humana-cvs-health-stocks-climb/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-06/medicare-finalizes-2-48-rate-hike-for-private-insurers-in-2027)
- **GLP-1 volume expansion.** The Medicare GLP-1 Bridge began **Jul 1, 2026**, offering Zepbound, Wegovy and oral Foundayo (orforglipron) at a $50/month copay. Eli Lilly crossed a **$1T market cap** in mid-September. [CMS](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coverage/prescription-drug-coverage/medicare-glp-1-bridge), [24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/09/02/lillys-record-stock-price-is-hiding-an-even-bigger-opportunity/)
- **M&A wave driven by the patent cliff.** Biotech M&A reached ~$106B across 201 deals by early June, on track for the best year since before Covid. Big pharma faces $150–300B of revenue losing exclusivity by 2030. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/04/biotech-ma-dealmaking-pharma-106-billion.html)
- **Earnings trajectory.** Health care and industrials are the only sectors where consensus expects **faster EPS growth in 2027 than in 2026**. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/analysis/sp-500-earnings-expected-2027-eps-growth-might-make-for-a-tougher-year-200687249)
- **Tools recovery.** Thermo Fisher and Danaher raised 2026 guidance on stronger bioprocessing orders. [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/thermo-fisher-raises-2026-outlook-153700819.html)
- **Flows, positioning and strategist views.**
  - Health care took in +$2.2B of ETF inflows in August, the biggest of any sector, while tech and financials lost $11B. [SSGA flows](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/pdfs/etf/us/monthly-flash-flows.pdf)
  - BofA FMS managers are rotating toward health care.
  - Schwab and Yardeni both hold favorable views. [Schwab](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/stock-sector-outlook), [Yardeni](https://www.yardeniquicktakes.com/us-sectors-call-energy-information-technology-health-care/)

### Against

- **6M relative return is slightly negative** (−1.8pp): XLV lagged badly during April's high-beta snap-back.
- **Q3 EPS estimates are being revised down** for health care (FactSet).
- **Sub-industry momentum has cooled since late July.** Over 3M, XPH is −0.4%, XBI −0.9% and IHF +0.3%. In September XPH fell 6.4% and IHI 5.6%. Leadership is migrating to large caps (IBB +11.2% over 3M, LLY), a sign of a maturing trend. [Stocktwits: XBI loses steam](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/biotech-midweek-pulse-xbi-loses-steam-hot-2026-stocks-catalysts-watch/cZtYGZfRB2R)
- **Part of the bid is defensive rotation.** It could fade if yields fall and high-beta risk appetite returns.

## 5. #2 Technology (semiconductor-led): strongest momentum and the biggest catalyst

### Price evidence

**Magnitude**

- XLK +52.9% and SMH +67.4% over 6M, vs SPY +21.5%.
- Best multi-horizon excess of any sector: +10.8pp on average.
- Beat SPY in 4 of the 6 monthly segments.

**Recent momentum**

- Semis fell as much as 29% from their late-June peak during the July selloff.
- Since then, XLK is +16.9% over 2M vs SPY +5.0%.
- In September, XLK rose 4.4% and SMH 9.0% while the 10-yr broke 5% and the S&P 500 was flat.
- The relative-strength line made a new high. Rising discount rates did not dent it, because earnings growth outweighed them.

**Software's different path**

- Software (IGV) was the best 3M performer among the large sub-industries: +17.1%, or +13.7pp vs SPY.
- It rebounded ~40%+ from its April "SaaSpocalypse" low as agentic-AI monetization eased disruption fears. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/software-is-up-40percent-since-saaspocalypse-bottom-ways-to-play-the-sector-.html)
- It gave back 4.3% in September.

### Catalysts

- **Nvidia (quarter ended Jul 26).**
  - Revenue $96.2B (+106% y/y); data center $89.0B (+117%).
  - Guided to $108B against ~$104B consensus.
  - Management says top-5 hyperscaler capex rises from ~$800B in 2026 to ~$1.3T in 2027, with supply constrained through fiscal 2028.
  - [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000073/q2fy27pr.htm), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2027-live-updates.html)
- **Memory up-cycle.** Micron reports fiscal Q4 on **Sep 30**; consensus revenue is ~$51B (≈+350% y/y). [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/know-ahead-micron-earnings-release-085748164.html)
- **Revisions.** IT is among the sectors with the largest upward Q3 EPS revisions (FactSet).

### Against

- **Volatility and air pockets.** Annualized volatility is 30% for XLK and 46% for SMH. The June–July episode cut semis by about 29% in weeks.
- **Narrowing.** In September, equal-weight tech rose only 1.0% vs XLK's 4.4%, and MAGS outpaced the market. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/26/meta-microsoft-led-tech-higher-this-week-despite-soaring-bond-yields.html)
- **Deceleration ahead.** Consensus expects tech to post the **steepest EPS-growth deceleration in 2027**. The acceleration metric is the most negative of any sector (−26pp).
- **Crowding and rates.** Tech and financials ETFs lost $11B in August. At a 5%+ 10-yr, multiples are the pressure point.

## 6. #3 Energy: strongest 3-month momentum, event-driven catalyst

### Price evidence

**Magnitude**

- Best 3M sector: +15.6% vs SPY +3.4% (+12.2pp).
- Confirmed by equal-weight (RSPG +10.3%) and E&P (XOP +15.4%).
- 12M: +40.2%. Best YTD sector (~+39–45% depending on source and date). [ChartRow](https://chartrow.com/sp500/sector-performance)

**Shape**

- Round trip: −17.3, −11.0 and −2.3pp vs SPY in the Apr–Jun segments as the ceasefire held.
- Then +11.0pp (Jul) and +3.9pp (Aug) after the ceasefire collapsed.
- Then −3.1pp in September. Oil services fell 11.4% and XOP 5.1%.
- XLE's 6M return is only +0.6%.

### Catalysts

- **Supply shock.** The 2026 Iran war and Strait of Hormuz disruption are the largest energy shock since the 1970s. The Jul 8 ceasefire collapse was followed by attacks on shipping and US naval assets in September. Brent was ~$104 and WTI ~$92 on Sep 25. [Wikipedia: ceasefire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war_ceasefire), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-iran-war.html), [EnergyNow](https://energynow.com/2026/09/oil-ends-week-lower-as-u-s-iran-truce-hopes-hit-wti-while-middle-east-supply-risks-keep-brent-above-100/)
- **Earnings.** Q3 EPS growth is estimated at **+111%**, the highest of any sector, with the largest upward revisions (FactSet). Exxon's Q2 profit roughly doubled to $14.5B. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2026/8/4/why-are-oil-companies-posting-record-profits-amid-iran-war-disruption)

### Why it ranks #3

- **Binary catalyst.** Truce headlines knocked WTI down 7.9% in the week to Sep 25, and energy equities fell with it.
- **Earnings are being treated as transitory.** Q3 EPS is expected to more than double, yet XLE is flat over 6M. The market is discounting the windfall rather than capitalizing it. The majors gave back 20%+ from their Q1 peaks when the spring ceasefire held. [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261937060-iran-us-oil-gas-exxonmobil-chevron-conocophillips-2026-stock-tradingkey)
- **Momentum is fading into quarter-end.** The 1M return is negative and oil services show the steepest drop.

## 9. Method notes and caveats

- **Price data.** Alpha Vantage `ANALYTICS_FIXED_WINDOW` gives cumulative return and annualized std-dev of daily closes, as price returns without dividends. Over 6M, omitting dividends understates high-yield sectors (XLU, XLRE, XLE, XLP) by roughly 0.5–1.5pp. That does not change any ranking.
- **Monthly path.** Built from nested windows sharing the Sep 29 end date: level at window start = 1/(1+R). "At period high" refers to the seven anchor dates, not intraday or daily highs.
- **What was not computed.** No daily bars were available, so there are no moving averages, 52-week-high distances or RSI.
  - The free Alpha Vantage key had hit its time-series daily cap.
  - The session's network policy blocked direct data hosts (finviz.com, stockanalysis.com, stooq.com, the Yahoo Finance API hosts, alphavantage.co).
- **Discarded API output.** `MAX_DRAWDOWN` was internally inconsistent: XLU showed −5% max drawdown alongside a −12% window return.
- **Catalyst evidence.** Drawn from secondary web summaries of FactSet, BofA FMS, company filings and news. Where a press claim conflicted with the price data, the price data won. For example, "XBI +55% since June 1" is contradicted by XBI's −0.9% over 3M.
- **Scope.** This is an evidence ranking. It is not a recommendation or a trading strategy.
